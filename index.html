I am an Assistant Professor of Marketing at the University of Iowa. My current research explores the cognition of modern language models to advance safety-aligned AI. I aim to build more resilient, interpretable systems that responsibly harness the power of AI, aligned with human values and for the benefit of both platforms and the communities they serve. Read more here.

I've received my PhD. in Quantitative Marketing with a focus on Causal ML & Digital Experimentation from UT Dallas. Outside of research, I keep myself busy with reading about philosophy, mythology, observing the sky and talking with my dog, Lili (mostly about her food and her research ideas).

Over the years, I've met incredible people who helped me understand where I want to be and how to get there. I'm always glad to pay it forward. Get in touch if I can be of any help!
